Yes. There are zero subscriptions, zero paywalls, zero ads, and zero credit card requirements. The app is built with an offline-first Room database, meaning it costs the developer \$0 to run and costs you \$0 to use.

Yes! You can put your phone on Airplane Mode and all features—including the Pomodoro timer, 6-phase adaptive schedule generator, daily session tracking, syllabus editor, and Scholar XP—will work 100% flawlessly.

Any Android phone or tablet running Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher up to Android 15. Tested thoroughly on Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, OnePlus, Xiaomi, and Motorola.
